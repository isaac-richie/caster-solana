# 🎯 PolyCaster - Tier Features Outline

## 📊 **TIER STRUCTURE: Growth Ladder**

### **FREE (Basic) Tier** - Current Implementation
**Target**: Casual users, beginners, free users

#### ✅ **Core Features (Currently Implemented)**
- ✅ **Market Browsing** - Browse all Polymarket markets
- ✅ **Search & Filter** - Search markets, filter by category
- ✅ **Watchlist** - Save up to 10 markets
- ✅ **Basic Price Alerts** - Up to 5 active alerts
- ✅ **Email Notifications** - Email alerts when price triggers
- ✅ **ROI Calculator** - Simple profit/loss calculator
- ✅ **Signal History** - View purchased AI signals
- ✅ **Mobile Responsive** - Full mobile support
- ✅ **Dark/Light Mode** - Theme toggle
- ✅ **Onboarding Tour** - Interactive tutorial
- ✅ **Help & FAQ** - Documentation and support

#### 📊 **Limitations**
- Watchlist: 10 markets max
- Price Alerts: 5 active alerts max
- AI Analysis: Pay per analysis (no free credits)
- Email Alerts: Basic email only
- No advanced analytics
- No portfolio tracking
- No export features

---

### **PRO Tier** - Power Users
**Target**: Active traders, serious market participants
**Pricing**: Subscription-based (monthly/annual)

#### 🚀 **All Free Features +**

##### **1. Advanced Watchlist**
- ✅ Unlimited saved markets
- ✅ Custom watchlist folders/categories
- ✅ Bulk operations (add/remove multiple)
- ✅ Watchlist sharing (export/import)
- ✅ Advanced notes & tags

##### **2. Enhanced Price Alerts**
- ✅ Unlimited active alerts
- ✅ Advanced alert conditions:
  - Price change percentage
  - Volume thresholds
  - Time-based alerts
  - Multiple conditions (AND/OR)
- ✅ Browser Push Notifications
- ✅ SMS/WhatsApp Alerts (optional)
- ✅ Alert templates & presets
- ✅ Alert history & analytics

##### **3. Portfolio Tracking**
- ✅ Track positions across markets
- ✅ Portfolio P&L dashboard
- ✅ Performance analytics
- ✅ Win/loss tracking
- ✅ ROI over time charts
- ✅ Export portfolio data (CSV/JSON)

##### **4. Advanced AI Analysis**
- ✅ Free AI analysis credits (e.g., 10/month)
- ✅ Priority AI processing (faster results)
- ✅ Advanced AI insights:
  - Risk assessment
  - Market sentiment analysis
  - Historical pattern matching
  - Confidence scoring
- ✅ AI analysis history & comparison
- ✅ Bulk analysis discounts

##### **5. Market Analytics**
- ✅ Market Scanner (advanced filters)
- ✅ Market Compare Tool (side-by-side comparison)
- ✅ Historical Charts & Trends
- ✅ Volume & Liquidity Analytics
- ✅ Market Correlation Analysis
- ✅ Custom market dashboards

##### **6. Signal Leaderboard**
- ✅ Trader Performance Rankings
- ✅ Copy Trading Signals
- ✅ Signal Success Rates
- ✅ Top Performer Insights
- ✅ Follow top traders

##### **7. Advanced Features**
- ✅ Market Bundles (group related markets)
- ✅ Daily/Weekly Market Digest (email)
- ✅ Custom Alerts (webhook support)
- ✅ API Access (limited)
- ✅ Data Export (CSV, JSON)
- ✅ Advanced Filters & Saved Searches

##### **8. Premium Support**
- ✅ Priority email support
- ✅ Feature request priority
- ✅ Early access to new features
- ✅ Dedicated help resources
